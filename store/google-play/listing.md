## Full description (max 4000)

ownit2buildit puts your construction company in your pocket. Site teams, supervisors, managers and finance staff use the same app, and each person sees only what their role allows.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Projects and tasks
- See your tasks and every project you work on, with status, dates, team and documents.
- Switch between tiles and a Gantt chart that fits your phone.
- Add photos, files and comments to a task, and update its status from site.

Time on site
- Clock in and out in a tap. Turn on location if you want where you clocked in recorded.
- Managers see who is on site, hours worked per project, the daily average and timesheets waiting for approval.

Materials, stock and purchasing
- Log materials used, count stock and correct it.
- Raise purchase requests, approve them, make purchase orders, receive deliveries and record direct purchases.
- Keep suppliers and a materials catalogue.

Budgets and money
- Track each project's budget against what is spent, with warnings when a category nears or passes its limit.
- See money in, costs and profit for a day, month or date range, by project, and open every payment and cost behind the figure.
- Create quotes and invoices, send them to clients and record cash or bank payments.

Approvals and changes
- Approve timesheets, purchase requests, quotes and change requests from your phone.
- Raise and review change requests with their cost and schedule effect.

Team chat
- Direct chats and project groups with photos, files, voice notes, replies and @mentions.
- Start a chat with a colleague or a client from their profile.

Stay informed
- Alerts for tasks assigned to you, time waiting for approval, new messages, budgets at their limit and invoices paid. Choose which kinds of alert you want.
- Light and dark mode.

Also: equipment, clients, team directory, company profile and reports.

GOOD TO KNOW
- You need an ownit2buildit account. Your company's administrator invites you; there is no sign-up in the app.
- What you can see and do depends on the permissions your company gives you.
- Location, microphone, camera and notifications are optional and asked for only when a feature needs them.
